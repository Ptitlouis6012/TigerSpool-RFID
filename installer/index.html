Install TigerSpool RFID

Plug the board into this computer, click Install, and wait about a minute. No toolchain, no command line.

This browser cannot flash a board. Installing needs WebSerial, which only Chrome, Edge and Opera implement, on a desktop or laptop. Safari, Firefox and every mobile browser are out. Open this page in one of those three, or build and flash it yourself.

What it writes

The bootloader, the partition table, the boot selector and the firmware — the same four images published with the release, at the same offsets scripts/flash.sh uses over USB.

Choose Erase only on a board you are setting up for the first time. Erasing wipes the NVS partition, which is where a TigerSpool keeps its Wi-Fi credentials, its TigerTag session and its imported printers. An ordinary install leaves all of that alone — that is what the two OTA slots are for.

After it reboots

  1. Pick your language.
  2. Scan the Wi-Fi QR code with your phone and join the network the box asks for.
  3. Sign in to your TigerTag account, by e-mail or with Google.

Your printers arrive from your account. If the list comes up empty, they have not been added in Tiger Studio Manager yet — that is where a printer is declared, and the box only reads what is there.

From then on the box updates itself over the air. This page is for the first install.
