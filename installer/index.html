Install TigerSpool RFID

Plug the board into this computer, press Install, and wait about a minute. No toolchain, no command line.

Firmware
1

What you need

Two boards, four wires and a cable that carries data.

  • A Waveshare ESP32-S3 2inch Capacitive Touch Display board.
  • A PN532 NFC module, V3, DIP switches both 0 / OFF, wired to GPIO44 and GPIO43 — never GPIO6/7. Four wires, and they come with the module. Wiring diagram.
  • A USB-C to USB-A cable that carries data, not a charge-only one.
  • Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop or laptop.
  • A TigerTag account, created in Tiger Studio Manager — see step 4.
2

Install

Writes the bootloader, the partition table, the boot selector and the firmware — the same four images published with the release.
This browser cannot flash a board.

Installing needs WebSerial, which only Chrome, Edge and Opera implement, on a desktop or laptop. Safari, Firefox and every mobile browser are out. Open this page in one of those three, or build and flash it yourself.

Choose Erase only on a board you are setting up for the first time.

Erasing wipes the NVS partition, which is where a TigerSpool keeps its Wi-Fi credentials, its TigerTag session and its imported printers. An ordinary install leaves all of that alone — that is what the two OTA slots are for.

No serial port offered?

The usual cause is a charge-only USB cable. The board lights up, the screen works, and no computer ever sees it. Try a cable you know transfers files.

3

Set it up on the box

  1. Pick your language. Eight of them, before anything else.
  2. Join Wi-Fi. The box shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone and a page opens listing the networks it can see — pick yours and type the password on the phone. Nothing is typed on the small screen, and the box does not reboot.
  3. Sign in to your TigerTag account, by e-mail or with Google through a second QR code.
  4. Your printers arrive from your account.

Then hold a tagged spool against the box, tap the slot you want, and confirm. From then on it updates itself over the air — this page is only for the first install.

4

If the printer list is empty

That is not a fault, and there is nothing to fix on the device. It is showing exactly what your account contains.

A TigerSpool has no keyboard and no way to be told a printer's address — that is deliberate. Printers are declared once, in Tiger Studio Manager, together with their access codes, and every TigerSpool you own reads the same list. Add the printer there and it appears on the box at the next sync.

  • Install Tiger Studio Manager and create your TigerTag account.
  • Add your printers to it.
  • Then sign in on the box.
